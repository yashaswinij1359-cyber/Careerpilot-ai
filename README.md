# 🚀 CareerPilot AI

### AI-Powered Career Guidance & Career Planning Platform

CareerPilot AI is an AI-powered career assistant designed to help students and job seekers plan, improve, and track their career journey.

It combines **AI career guidance, skill analysis, project recommendations, learning roadmaps, and interview preparation** in one simple platform.

---

## 🌟 Features

### 🤖 AI Career Assistant
Ask questions about your career, skills, learning path, projects, interviews, and job preparation.

### 🎯 Career Planning
Set your target career role and create a personalized career direction based on your goals.

### 📊 Skill Analysis
Add your current skills and track your progress to identify areas that need improvement.

### 🛣️ Personalized Roadmap
Get a structured learning roadmap to move from your current skill level toward your target role.

### 💡 Project Recommendations
Discover project ideas that can help you build practical experience and strengthen your portfolio.

### 🎤 Interview Preparation
Prepare for technical and career-related interviews with AI-powered questions and feedback.

### 📈 Progress Tracking
Monitor your career progress and keep track of your skills and learning journey.

### 🔐 Secure API Integration
The Gemini API key is stored securely using environment variables and is not exposed in the frontend.
